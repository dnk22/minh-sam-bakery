## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the poetic intersection of traditional Vietnamese culinary craftsmanship and contemporary luxury publishing. Designed specifically for an esteemed artisanal bakery based in Hanoi, the interface balances cultural nostalgia with clean modern restraint. 

The emotional tone evokes the warm stillness of an Old Quarter tea salon: the tactile richness of aged lacquerware, hand-carved wooden pastry molds, seasonal botanical aromas, and moonlit celebrations. It strictly avoids loud, commercial scarlet reds and discount-driven retail interfaces in favor of a quiet, deliberate gastronomic journey.

### Design Movements & Philosophy
- **Cinematic Culinary Editorial:** Product photography acts as architectural focal points, flanked by generous negative space and thoughtful asymmetric grids.
- **Understated Heritage Tactility:** Fine gilded hairlines, subtle imperial crest seals, and paper-textured background surfaces evoke craft and heirloom value.
- **Harmonious Bilingual Rhythm:** Typography gracefully handles the complex diacritical marks of Vietnamese script without visual crowding, preserving typographic rhythm alongside Latin text.

## Colors

The palette draws its soul from the natural materials of northern Vietnamese pastry making: deep oxblood lacquer, aged brass accents, dried tea leaves, and steamed rice paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#4A151B` / `#3B0E14`):** Imperial Oxblood. Used for primary interactive actions, high-impact headlines, hero backdrops, and signature brand markers. It replaces aggressive retail red with an aristocratic, slow-aged wine tone.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / `#DFBD74`):** Muted Antique Gold. Applied to delicate border hairlines, insignia badges, micro-eyebrows, and decorative separators. It brings warmth without overt sheen.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6553`):** Hanoi Green Tea & Dried Lotus Leaf. An earthy olive sage tone reserved for seasonal badges, origin markers, and ingredients (such as cốm làng Vòng or pandan leaf accents).
- **Neutral & Body (`#241614` / `#382A28`):** Roasted Espresso Brown. Serves as deep typography neutral, replacing sterile stark black with an organic, wood-smoke warmth.
- **Canvas & Surfaces (`#FBF9F4` / `#F5EFEB` / `#F0EAE1`):** Warm Ivory & Silk Parchment. Luminous, organic light surfaces that provide breathing room for food photography and gilded line work.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy rests upon a deliberate dialogue between classical serif gravitas and crisp contemporary legibility.

### Font Roles
- **Noto Serif:** Serves for headline and editorial narrative tiers. It is chosen for its native, balanced diacritic rendering in Vietnamese (dấu hỏi, ngã, sắc, huyền, nặng align gracefully with cap-height and ascenders without vertical clipping or awkward spacing).
- **Be Vietnam Pro:** Engineered specifically for the Vietnamese language, its proportions ensure that diacritics maintain crisp distinction even at micro label sizes and dense navigation headers.

### Typographic Guidelines
- All section categories utilize `label-eyebrow` tracked at wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`) paired with antique gold tones to announce content gracefully.
- Editorial introductions employ `subheading` italicized styling to emulate literary cookbook notes.
- Line heights in body copy remain generous (`1.55` to `1.65`) to maintain air around accented vowels (`ơ`, `ư`, `ê`, `â`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an exhibition catalogue: content is framed with architectural discipline, never crowded.

### Grid System & Responsive Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `gutter-desktop` (32px) and canvas outer margins set to `margin-desktop` (80px). Maximum content containment is bounded at 1360px to preserve intentional white borders.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 40px outer margins. Multi-column editorial cards reflow into balanced 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margin. Cards drop into vertical flow or deliberate horizontal peek carousels with visible slide counters (e.g. `01 / 03`).

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections are spaced with deep vertical breathing room (80px to 120px on desktop), allowing full bleed photography and hero text blocks to evoke stillness.
- Internal component rhythm follows an 8px base module, using `space-xs` through `space-xl` for card paddings and interactive gap controls.

## Elevation & Depth

In keeping with artisanal lacquered craft and handmade paper surfaces, spatial separation relies on tactile surfaces and delicate line work rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Elevation Principles
- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation is achieved primarily through a 1px boundary in antique gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) or soft espresso tint (`rgba(36, 22, 20, 0.08)`).
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual elevation is created through subtle parchment tiering:
  - Base Page Canvas: `#FBF9F4`
  - Container / Card Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F2EC`
  - Featured / Premium Drawer: Deep Oxblood `#3B0E14`
- **Soft Ambient Vignette:** When floating elements require physical lift (such as sticky reservation bars, quick-view sheets, or cart drawers), use a diffused warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(36, 22, 20, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a refined, nearly crisp contour language (Level 1: Soft). 

### Geometry & Motif Language
- **Soft Geometry:** A subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius softens corners on cards, interactive buttons, and imagery without sacrificing classical architectural structure.
- **Pill Outlines:** Reserved strictly for micro tags, category filters, and editorial badge indicators to echo traditional oval seal stamps (*triện son*).
- **Circular Seals:** Brand medallions, quick-add icons, and numeric carousel indicators adopt perfect circles to echo mooncake wooden stamps and full-moon motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero Button:** Oxblood background (`#4A151B`), warm ivory text (`#FBF9F4`), subtle 1px border in antique gold (`#C5A059`), with a gentle 4px radius. Hover state lifts with a deep lacquer sheen and slight brightness shift (`#5E1C24`).
- **Editorial Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid hairline in `#C5A059` or `#4A151B`, accompanied by a minimal directional arrow (`→`) and tracked label typography. On hover, fills with a sheer 8% gold or oxblood wash.
- **Compact Icon Button:** Circular 36px interactive target with a 1px hairline border, housing delicate monoline iconography (e.g. plus icon, search, bookmark).

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Heritage Pastry Card:** Features a 1:1 or 4:5 ratio food photograph framed in warm ivory, bounded by a 1px border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`). Displays product title in `Noto Serif`, tasting notes in `Be Vietnam Pro`, price tag in dark espresso, and an unobtrusive circular `+` quick-select icon in the bottom corner.
- **Collection Showcase Card:** Displays an architectural serial index (e.g. `01`, `02`), large photographic preview, category heading, and an itemized ingredient list beneath, terminating in a ghost link.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Parchment Input:** Background tinted in `#F5EFEB`, border defined by a 1px bottom rule or delicate full frame in `#DFBD74`, body text in espresso brown. Focused state transitions the border to solid oxblood (`#4A151B`) with no generic browser glow.
- **Footer Newsletter Input:** Dark espresso lacquer field (`#1B100E`) framed with a 1px muted gold line and an inline directional submission arrow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Attribute Chip:** Small capsule shape with muted olive background (`rgba(90, 101, 83, 0.1)`) and deep sage text, indicating certifications like "Hương vị Cốm Làng Vòng" or "Thủ Công Gia Truyền".
- **Origin Badge:** Circular or bordered label with hairline gold detail used to denote limited Mid-Autumn festival editions (*Mùa Trăng*).

### Navigation & Header
- **Minimalist Header:** Floats seamlessly over the hero imagery with translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(27, 16, 14, 0.65)` in dark hero mode or `#FBF9F4` in light mode). Anchored by the central brand crest and flanked by uppercase spaced navigation links.
